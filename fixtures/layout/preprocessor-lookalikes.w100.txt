▌▌ Divs a reader is being shown
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For example, here we add the "border" class to a region of content using a div (:::):

::: {.border}                                                                                       
This content can be styled with a border                                                            
:::                                                                                                 

Divs may also be nested, and the outer one takes more colons:

::::: {#special .sidebar}                                                                           
                                                                                                    
::: {.warning}                                                                                      
Here is a warning.                                                                                  
:::                                                                                                 
                                                                                                    
More content.                                                                                       
:::::
